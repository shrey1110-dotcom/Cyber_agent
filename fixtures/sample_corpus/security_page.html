Network Service Review

A listening port is evidence that a process has created a socket; it is not proof that the service is externally reachable.

Review the owning process, bind address, transport protocol, firewall policy, and expected system role before deciding whether a listener is suspicious.

Record the observation and use an approved change process before disabling a production service.
